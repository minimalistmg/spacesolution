# Space Solutions — 3 redesign variants (research brief)

## Research references

**Award-winning interiors / architecture sites (Awwwards SOTD):** MERSI, Nine To Five, Studio X, Telha Clarke, Studio Dado.

**Top immersive agencies (US + global luxury):** Noomo (Los Angeles), The First The Last (Miami), Digital Luxury Group, 14islands, Immersive Garden (Awwwards Agency of the Year 2025).

## Borrowed design elements

- Cinematic full-bleed heroes with scroll-triggered typography reveals (GSAP / ScrollTrigger cues)
- Sticky section indexes and numbered chapter navigation
- Oversized editorial type with generous whitespace
- Project galleries as immersive case strips / masonry / filmstrips
- Soft glass / frosted panels over photography
- Process as architectural timeline
- Luxury enquiry forms as focused destination experiences
- Motion suggested via staggered cards, progress lines, and hover filmstrips

## Variant concepts

| Folder | Concept | Inspiration blend |
|--------|---------|-------------------|
| `variant-1` | **Cinematic Editorial** | Immersive Garden / Studio Dado / Cartier-level dark maison storytelling |

| `variant-3` | **Immersive Flagship** | Noomo / The First The Last — video-first, glass depth, filmstrip galleries, conversion |
